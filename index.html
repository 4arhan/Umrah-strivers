Guide & settings

How the app works, essential duas, and your data.

📘 How to use this app

🧳 Plan — before you fly ▶
Set your departure date for a live countdown. Work through the preparation checklists (documents, packing, health, knowledge). Read every "Know Before You Go" topic — miqats, ihram rules, permits, transport and common mistakes. Then take the quiz until you score 80%+.
🕋 Umrah — during the rites ▶
The Umrah tab walks you through every step from ihram to halq, with the duas inline. Tap the big Tawaf and Sa'i circles after each round — with haptic feedback — so you never lose count. Then record your completed Umrah; the steps reset for your next one.
📿 Daily — every day in the Haramain ▶
Tick off your five congregational prayers, tahajjud, Quran, dhikr and extra deeds. The app tracks streaks, a consistency heatmap, points and achievements. Set your trip length in Settings.
📍 Places — ziyarah ▶
Every major site in Makkah and Madinah with why it matters, an etiquette tip and one-tap Google Maps navigation. Ziyarah is a virtuous extra — the rites and prayers always come first.
🔒 Privacy & data ▶
Everything lives on your device only — no accounts, no servers, no tracking. Export a backup anytime; the app works offline in the crowded Haram where signal drops.
