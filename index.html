📘 How to Use This App

Tap any section to expand it

🧳 Plan — before you fly ▶
Set your departure date for a live countdown. Work through the preparation checklists (documents, packing, health, knowledge). Read every "Know Before You Go" topic — miqats, ihram rules, permits, transport, and common mistakes. Then take the quiz until you score 80%+. A prepared heart makes a focused Umrah.
🕋 Umrah — during the rites ▶
The Umrah tab walks you through every step from ihram to halq, with the duas you need inline. Use the big Tawaf and Sa'i counters while you walk — one tap per round, with haptic feedback, so you never lose count. When you finish, tap "Record Completed Umrah" — it logs your Umrah and resets the steps for your next one (take ihram from Masjid Aisha in Tan'eem).
📿 Daily — maximise every day ▶
While you're in the Haramain, every prayer is multiplied — don't waste a single day. Tick off your five prayers in congregation, tahajjud, Quran, dhikr and extra deeds. The app tracks streaks, a consistency heatmap, points, and unlocks achievements. Set your trip length in Settings below.
📍 Places — ziyarah with purpose ▶
Every major site in Makkah and Madinah, each with why it matters, an etiquette tip, and a one-tap Google Maps button for navigation. Mark places as visited to track your ziyarah progress. Note: ziyarah is a virtuous extra — the Umrah rites and prayers in the Haramain always come first.
🔒 Privacy & Data ▶
All your data lives on your device only. No accounts, no servers, no tracking. Export a backup anytime and import it on another device. The app works offline — essential in the crowded Haram where signal drops.
