Before the checklist — the intention

Umrah is answered by the heart before it is booked by the hands. Three foundations, then everything else.

1 · The Intention (Niyyah)

Define your specific spiritual goals for this journey — what you are asking Allah for, what you want to leave behind. "Umrah to Umrah is an expiation for what is between them" (Bukhari 1773) — minor sins; major sins need sincere tawbah. Go purely for Allah, not for the photos.

2 · The Knowledge (Fiqh)

Learning the rituals is mandatory. Do not obsess over the hotel and forget the rules of the worship. The Learn and Quiz tabs exist for this — finish the circuit before you fly.

3 · The Detachment

Cut ties with worldly distractions before departure — markets, debates, idle talk, the scroll. Settle debts, seek forgiveness from people, write your will, and travel light in the heart.

Write it down — it becomes your compass for the whole trip.
