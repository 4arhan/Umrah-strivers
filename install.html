A look inside

Common questions

Is it really free?
Yes, completely. No purchase, no subscription, no advertising and no paid tier. It was built as sadaqah jariyah.
Does it work without signal in the Haram?
Yes. The rites, the duas, all 51 places, the driver cards and even the prayer times work with the network off. Prayer times are cached a month ahead and can be calculated on the device if nothing is cached.
Where is my data stored?
Only on your phone. There is no account and no server. You can export a backup file or erase everything from Settings at any time. Read the privacy policy.
Is the religious content reliable?
Every hadith citation is checked against its collection. Paraphrases are kept faithful, and weak narrations are avoided or marked. It is a study and tracking aid, not a substitute for asking a scholar about your own situation.
Do I need to install it at all?
It runs in any modern browser, but installing it is the safe way to keep your data. On an iPhone, Safari can clear the data of a website you have not opened for a week; an app added to the Home Screen is kept. Links opened inside Instagram, Facebook or other apps use a separate browser that cannot install the app and keeps its data apart, so open the link in Safari or Chrome first. Installing also gives you an icon and a full screen without the browser bars, which matters most in the mataf.
